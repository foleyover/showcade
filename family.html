📜 Final Family Game Rules

🎮 Main Board Round

Two families face off over survey prompts answered by 100 people. The controlling family guesses survey answers to load points into the Round Bank. Reveal answers directly or use the Host Dashboard!

❌ Three Strikes & Steals

Incorrect answers award a Strike (X). If the controlling family racks up 3 strikes, the opposing family gets one single guess to find any remaining answer on the board and steal the entire Round Bank!

⚡ How Fast Money Starts

Fast Money is the bonus finale played only by the winning family after the main game concludes. It unlocks automatically when a family reaches the target score (default 300 pts), or after all main survey rounds are played!

⚡ Fast Money Rules

The winning family chooses 2 players. Player 1 gets 20 seconds to answer 5 rapid-fire questions while Player 2 is out of earshot (or muted). Player 2 gets 25 seconds to answer the same questions. If they combine for 200 points, they win the jackpot!
