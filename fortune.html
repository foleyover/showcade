📜 Box of Fortune Game Rules & Mechanics

🔄 Turn Rotation & The Spin Lock

The game tracks turns automatically based on player order. The active player is highlighted on the leaderboard and turn tracker. The Guess Letter button is locked (greyed out) at the start of a turn and requires the host to spin the wheel first before a letter can be selected.

🎲 Wheel Spins & Outcomes

Clicking Random Spin pulls a weighted value from the wheel. Cash values multiply against correct consonant guesses. Bankrupt wipes a player's score to $0 and ends their turn, while Lose a Turn simply passes the turn. Both have reduced frequencies to make cash wins more common!

🔤 Consonants vs. Vowels

Consonants: Correct guesses multiply the active spin value by the number of times that letter appears in the puzzle and keep the player's turn going.
Vowels (A, E, I, O, U): Cost a fixed price (default $250) to buy. They do not earn cash when revealed. If a player does not have enough money, the vowel buttons are automatically greyed out and unavailable.

🎯 Solving the Puzzle

When a player figures out the phrase, click Solve Puzzle!. A modal will prompt you to choose the winning player, awarding them the custom Solve Bonus and automatically flipping open the rest of the board.

💩 The CHUD Button

Enabled in settings, click this when a player is acting argumentative. Selecting a player marks them as a CHUD, applying a strict penalty on their next correct/incorrect guess (Half points if correct, double penalty if incorrect).
