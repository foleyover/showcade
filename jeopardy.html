📜 Game Rules & Mechanics

👥 Players & Guesses

Configure Max Guesses per player. During the game, you can open the Leaderboard and click on any score to manually correct a value.

🎲 Daily Doubles

You can choose between Fixed Double Points or Wager (Standard).
How Wagers Work: Official rules apply! Players can risk up to their current score OR the highest value on the board, whichever is larger. Just select a percentage and the game does the math automatically.

🚨 Final Jeopardy & Scores

When you click 🚨 Final Jeopardy, the classic theme music plays! Use the secret wager panel to select each player's wager percentage. Click the winning player to calculate the scores and launch the cinematic Final Scores sequence.

↩️ Undo & Correction

Accidentally clicked the wrong button? An Undo Last Question button appears after every question to instantly revert scores and place the tile back on the board.

💻 Technical Guide & Media

📂 Supported Media Formats & Fast Uploads

✨ Drag & Drop + Clipboard Paste: You can drag images, audio, or web URLs straight onto any question box. Taking screenshots with Win+Shift+S or Cmd+Shift+4 and pressing Ctrl+V will paste image data automatically!

🎬 Videos: .mp4, .webm, .ogg, and YouTube Links.

🎵 Audio: .mp3, .wav.

🖼️ Images: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .gif, .webp, .svg.

☁️ Cloud Storage Links

Standard "Share" links point to a web viewer page, which won't load. You must use public direct links:

  • Google Drive: Set to "Anyone with the link can view". Take your file ID and use this format: https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=FILE_ID
  • OneDrive: Open the video, click the three dots (`...`), select Embed, and copy the src="..." URL string.

🌐 Hosting & Local Files

Local Videos: Web video URLs will not work if you open this file directly from your computer hard drive due to browser security restrictions.

Recommended Hosts: Host your game for free using GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages to make all YouTube embeds and online media stream seamlessly.
