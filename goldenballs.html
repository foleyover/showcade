📜 Golden Balls: Game Rules & Mechanics

🔒 1. Front & Back Rows

Each player starts with cash balls and Killers[cite: 1]. Front Row balls are revealed publicly to build the bank[cite: 1]. Back Row balls remain secret on private cards until carried over or lost[cite: 1].

💀 2. Killer Balls

Revealing a Killer Ball during the front row reveal (or keeping one in the finale) cuts the accumulated jackpot in half (50% penalty)[cite: 1].

🗳️ 3. Bluffing & Voting

Convince others you have high cash and zero Killers. Players DM secret votes to the host[cite: 1]. The most-voted player is eliminated along with their balls[cite: 1].

🎯 4. Bin or Keep (Final 2)

The final two select from 10 neutral balls: 5 are Kept to finalize the jackpot, and 5 are Binned safely[cite: 1].

🤝 5. Split or Steal Finale

Both SPLIT: Both finalists share the prize fund equally (50 / 50)[cite: 1].
One STEALS, One SPLITS: The Stealer takes 100% of the jackpot; the Splitter leaves with £0[cite: 1].
Both STEAL: Greed destroys the jackpot; both players leave empty-handed (£0)[cite: 1].

🕵️ Host Dashboard Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Download & DM Cards

Click 📥 Download All Player Cards to DM[cite: 1]. The game generates PNG images showing each player's secret front and back balls[cite: 1]. Send each player their card privately.

Step 2: Auto-Reveal Front Balls

Click ⚡ Auto-Reveal Public Front Balls[cite: 1]. The main screen will flip all public front row balls one by one, adding cash or slashing the pot if killers appear[cite: 1].

Step 3: Collect & Input Votes

Have players debate and privately DM their elimination votes. Use the dropdown menus for each player to input who they voted for, then click 🎬 Reveal Votes on Stream[cite: 1].

Step 4: Post-Vote Reveals & Banish

Use 👁️ Reveal Secret Balls to reveal hidden back rows for discussion[cite: 1]. When finished, click 🚪 Banish & Start Next Round to advance[cite: 1].

Step 5: The Final 2 (Bin/Keep & Split or Steal)

  • Bin or Keep: In the dashboard, click Keep (to add to bank or halve with killer) or Bin (discard) until 5 balls are kept[cite: 1].
  • Split or Steal: Collect finalists' secret choices, set both dropdowns in the dashboard, and click 🎬 Reveal Decisions on Stream[cite: 1].
