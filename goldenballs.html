📜 Golden Balls Game Rules & Mechanics

🔒 Persistent Golden Balls

4 players start with 4 balls each. Survived unrevealed balls carry forward and get reshuffled. Penultimate 3-player round adds 1 Killer (max 5); Final 2-player round adds 1 Killer (max 6).

🗳️ Secret Voting & Elimination

Host records secret votes on the Host Dashboard. The stream modal reveals the voted names and highlights the most-voted player. Ties remain in a deadlock state until re-voted from the Host Dashboard.

🤝 Split or Steal Finale

The final two submit their secret Split or Steal decisions to the host. The host triggers the reveal, showing choices before crowning the winner.
